## 2. Technology Route: LFP vs Solid-State

### 2.1 LFP global share (装机份额)
- **LFP passed ~55% of global EV battery deployment in 2025**, up from ~50% in 2024; total 2025 global EV battery deployment ~**1.2 TWh** (+~30% y/y). [IEA Global EV Outlook 2026, via evseekers.com, 2026-07-20]
- 2024 full-year shares: CATL 37.9%, BYD 17.2% (global EV battery usage). [SNE Research via China Daily HK, 2025-05-13]
- Q1 2026: global usage 244.6 GWh (+9.1% y/y); CATL 99.5 GWh (**40.7% share**); BYD 33.5 GWh (-8.0%); Korean trio (LGES/SK On/Samsung SDI) combined **15.6%** (-2.1pp). Jan–May 2026: CATL 40.2%, CATL+BYD 54.6%; seven of top 10 makers Chinese, 72.2% combined (Jan–Apr 2026). [SNE Research, 2026-05-06; SNE via evseekers/CnEVPost, 2026]
- LFP context: ~90% of the US BESS market used Chinese LFP cells in 2024. [Rho Motion's Iola Hughes via energy-storage.news, 2025-02-28]

### 2.2 LFP fast-charging flagships
- **CATL Shenxing family**: Gen-1 (Aug 2023, first 4C LFP, 700 km); Shenxing PLUS (Apr 2024, 4C+, 1,000 km, 205 Wh/kg system); **Gen-2 (Apr 2025 Super Tech Day): 800 km range + 12C peak rate, >1.3 MW peak power, 520 km per 5-min charge, 5–80% in 15 min at -10°C**; applied in 39 models/16 companies by end-2024, expanding to 67+ models; Shenxing Pro shown for Europe at IAA Munich Sept 2025. Gen-3 Shenxing + Gen-3 Qilin + Qilin Condensed Battery unveiled at **Super Tech Day 2026-04-21** (1,000 cycles with >90% retention; condensed battery 500 Wh/kg validated on 4-tonne aircraft). [Global X ETFs report, 2025-07-30; BatteryTech Online, 2025-05-07; CATL newsroom, 2026-04-21]
- **BYD Blade 2.0 / Super e-Platform (March 2025)**: 1000V architecture, megawatt flash charging — 400 km per 5 min at launch (Han L/Tang L, RMB 270–280k); 4,000+ 1-MW flash-charging piles planned. Later reporting describes **1,500 kW peak, 10–97% in 9 min**, evolved LMFP cells, ~50% lower internal resistance, 3D refrigerant cooling (~90% better heat dissipation), >1.3m km durability. Note: credible analysis holds Blade 2.0 remains LFP-family (LMFP variant). [Global X ETFs, 2025-07-30; sinexcel-re.com, 2026-05-06; batterydesign.net, 2026-07-30]
- **LMFP**: identified as the upgrade path from LFP (BYD layered-coating patents to mitigate manganese dissolution); CATL/SMM note high-compaction-density LFP in "undersupply" as fast-charging competition intensifies (2025 demand est. ~800 kt). [batterydesign.net; SMM, 2026-05-13]

### 2.3 Sodium-ion (CATL Naxtra 钠新)
- Launched **Apr 2025** (Super Tech Day): first mass-production-intent Na-ion EV battery; **175 Wh/kg**; 500-km range claim; >10,000 cycles; ~90% capacity at -40°C; first Na-ion battery to pass **GB 38031-2025** (effective 2026-07-01); ~30% cheaper than LFP at scale; Zeng: Na-ion could replace **30–40%** of the battery market long-term. [energyefficiency.ie, 2025-04-27; electrichybridvehicletechnology.com, 2026-05-11]
- Mass production timeline: mass production of Naxtra confirmed to begin **by end-2026** (some 2025 reports cited Dec 2025 for initial products); cumulative CATL Na-ion R&D ~RMB 10bn by end-2025. [electrichybridvehicletechnology, 2026-05-11; enkiai, 2026-04-28]
- Commercialization: **Feb 5 2026 — CATL+Changan unveiled world-first mass-produced Na-ion passenger EV (Nevo A06, 45 kWh Naxtra, >400 km, market by mid-2026)**; Tectrans II LCV Na-ion launched; Na-ion Aion UT Super (w/ GAC+JD.com) Q2 2026; **Apr 27 2026: record 60 GWh 3-yr Na-ion supply deal with HyperStrong (ESS)**; **May 2026: RMB 5bn Fuding expansion adding 40 GWh/yr Na-ion capacity** (total Fuding 149 GWh). [databridgemarketresearch, 2026-07-06; electrichybridvehicletechnology, 2026-05-11]

### 2.4 Solid-state batteries (固态电池)
**China state program & standards**
- **2024: MIIT-led ~RMB 6bn all-solid-state R&D special fund** supporting six players — CATL, BYD, FAW, SAIC, WeLion, Geely (CATL reportedly received RMB 2bn+, ~half); seven sub-projects across polymer/sulfide routes; mid-term review held Sept 2025; second tranche expected. [China Daily via chedongxi/autohome, 2024-06-03; Sina Finance (industry research), 2025-06-26; Xueqiu, 2025-09-05]
- May 2025: China Society of Automotive Engineers issued **T/CSAE 434-2025 "全固态电池判定方法"** — world's first group standard defining all-solid-state batteries. Apr 2025 MIIT standards work plan includes all-solid-state. Local funds: Zhuhai RMB 5bn, Shanghai RMB 3bn; NDRC ultra-long-term bonds subsidize solid-state projects at ~15% of investment (H2 2025 second round). [锂电设备行业报告, 2025-08-11; 微信公众号产业分析, 2025-09-07]

## 3. Geopolitics & Policy

### 3.1 United States

**FEOC / IRA baseline**
- DOE interpretive guidance: FEOC = entities headquartered/incorporated in China, PRC SOEs, or subject to **≥25% PRC government control** (voting/board/equity); excludes FEOC-linked battery components (from 2024) and critical minerals (from 2025) from the §30D credit. Graphite anode tracing exemption runs through **2026-12-31**. [TSM Hub summarizing DOE guidance, 2026-07-05]
- §45X advanced manufacturing production credit ($35/kWh cell, $10/kWh module, 10% critical minerals) survived OBBBA but with new PFE restrictions (below).

**OBBBA ("One Big Beautiful Bill Act", signed 2025-07-04)**
- **§30D/§25E/§45W EV credits terminated 2025-09-30**; §30C mid-2026; residential §25D ended 2025-12-31; §48E/§45Y wind/solar credits unavailable if construction starts after 2026-07-04 (placed-in-service by end-2027 otherwise); battery storage keeps credits for construction through 2035 but faces FEOC supply-chain risk. [C2ES, 2025-09-05; Deloitte 2026 Renewable Outlook, 2025-10-28; peony.ink, 2026-04-24]
- **New "prohibited foreign entity" (PFE) regime** extends FEOC-style restrictions to §45X, §45Y, §48E, §45Q, §45Z, §45U: (1) PFEs cannot claim credits (45X/45Y/48E/45Q effective immediately); (2) "**material assistance** cost ratio" thresholds disqualify credits if too much value derives from PFEs (applies from 2026-01-01 for projects; 45X tax years after enactment); (3) **licensing-agreement restrictions — credits denied where projects use technology/patents licensed from FEOCs** (directly threatening Ford-CATL LRS-type structures' 45X eligibility); (4) restrictions on financial arrangements/payments to PFEs. Example cited: Gotion = Specified Foreign Entity → its Michigan/Illinois output ineligible for 45X. [C2ES, 2025-09-05; Arnold & Porter, 2025-07-22; Bipartisan Policy Center, 2025-07-28; Baker Tilly via TSM Hub, 2025-07-30]
- 45X mechanics: wind-component credits end after 2027; integrated-component rule — from 2027, ≥65% (some summaries say 60%) US-sourced direct material cost required for integrated components sold after 2026-12-31; critical-minerals credit phases down 2031 (75%) → 2034 (0%); "battery module" definition tightened; transferability (§6418) survives (transfers to SFEs prohibited). [Arnold & Porter, 2025-07-22; C2ES, 2025-09-05]

**Tariffs on Chinese batteries/materials**
- Section 301 four-year review (final mods announced 2024-09-13, effective 2024-09-27): Chinese **EVs 100%**; **Li-ion EV batteries 7.5%→25%**; battery parts →25%; certain critical minerals 0→25%; solar cells 50%. From **2026-01-01**: **Li-ion non-EV batteries →25%**; **natural graphite →25%**; **permanent magnets →25%**. [China Briefing, updated 2026-07-28; customsandinternationaltradelaw.com]
- 2025 IEEPA tariffs: two additional 10% tariffs (Feb/Mar 2025) pushed the combined tariff on Chinese batteries/BESS toward **48.4% from Jan 2026** (3.4% base + 25% 301 + 2×10% IEEPA). [Energy-Storage.news, 2025-02-28]
- 2026 trade-law flux (corroborated mainly by secondary trackers — treat with caution): Supreme Court ruled (Feb 2026, 6–3) IEEPA "reciprocal" tariffs unlawful (refund pool reported); Section 232 25% auto/parts tariffs (since Apr/May 2025) unaffected; a 10% "Section 122" tariff applied Feb 24–~Jul 24 2026, replaced by a Section 301 forced-labor tariff (10–12.5%); EU-US deal 15% all-inclusive ceiling from 2026-07-01. [thechargeport.com, 2026-06-19; tariffstool.com, 2026-07-24 — **medium-low authority; verify before publication**]

### 3.2 European Union

**EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542** (in force 2023-08-17; applying since 2024-02-18)
- **Carbon footprint declaration (Art. 7)**: nominal dates — EV batteries 2025-02-18, rechargeable industrial >2 kWh 2026-02-18, LMT 2028-08-18, external-storage industrial 2030-08-18. **BUT each date = nominal date OR 12–18 months after the methodology delegated act enters into force, whichever is later; the EV methodology act (due 2024-02-18) is still a draft as of mid-2026 → the EV declaration obligation has NOT yet legally started.** Performance classes (nominal Aug 2026 EV) and maximum thresholds (nominal Feb 2028 EV) slide the same way. [traceable.digital, 2026-08-01; passfolia.com, 2026-07-10; Ramboll, 2025-10-27; DEKRA, 2026-02-05]
- **Battery Passport (Art. 77)**: mandatory **2027-02-18** for EV batteries, LMT batteries, industrial batteries >2 kWh; QR-code access; Annex XIII data (carbon footprint, recycled content, SoH, due-diligence declarations) with tiered public/legitimate-interest/restricted access. This date is fixed in the regulation (not delegated-act dependent). [scantrust.com, 2026-07-29; asuene.com, 2026-05-08]
- **Due diligence (Arts. 47–48, cobalt/natural graphite/lithium/nickel)**: originally 2025-08-18 → **postponed to 2027-08-18 by Regulation (EU) 2025/1561 (adopted July 2025, Omnibus IV)**; Commission guidelines due by 2026-07-26; threshold €40m net turnover; pending Omnibus IV proposal would exempt "small mid-caps" <€150m and move reporting to every 3 years (not yet adopted). Third-party notified-body verification required; 10-year record retention. [complianceandrisks.com, 2026-06-14; batterydesign.net, 2026-07-06; asuene.com]
- Recycled content: documentation from 2028-08-18; minimums from 2031-08-18 (Co 16%, Li 6%, Ni 6%, Pb 85%) and 2036 (Co 26%, Li 12%, Ni 15%). Labelling (capacity/chemistry/hazardous substances + QR) from 2026-08-18 (or 18 months after format act). [compliance-atlas.com, 2026-06-08; certivo.com, 2026-07-31]

**EU anti-subsidy duties on Chinese BEVs**
- Oct 2024: definitive 5-year countervailing duties on top of 10% MFN: **BYD 17.0%, Geely 18.8%, SAIC 35.3%, other cooperating companies 20.7%** (max combined ~45.3%). [Gasgoo/autonews, 2026-06-26]
- Apr 2025: China-EU agreed to negotiate **price undertakings (minimum import price)** as tariff alternative; **2026-01-12: framework consensus reached** with EC guidance for exporter applications; implementation still pending as of June 2026. Meanwhile (June 2026) EC reportedly **preparing anti-subsidy duties on Chinese-made PHEVs** (previously untouched) pending member-state approval. Chinese brands' EU market share hit record 9.8% (Apr 2026; >15% of BEV segment). [Gasgoo/autonews, 2026-06-26]

### 3.3 China export controls (出口管制)
- **2025-07-15 (effective immediately)**: MOFCOM + MOST amended the Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited/Restricted from Export — **LFP and LMFP cathode preparation technology restricted** (plus phosphate cathode raw materials), spodumene-based lithium carbonate/hydroxide production, brine/DLE lithium extraction, metal lithium/alloy preparation, and gallium extraction. "Restricted" = two-step MOFCOM license (preliminary license pre-contract + formal license pre-transfer); covers JVs, licensing, tech-service agreements. Followed a Jan 2025 consultation draft. [Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer, 2025-07-25; electrive, 2025-07-15; JD Supra, 2025-08-05]
- **2025-10-09 (Announcement No. 58, MOFCOM/GACC; effective 2025-11-08)**: export controls on **lithium batteries (≥300 Wh/kg), cathode materials, artificial graphite anode materials, and related manufacturing equipment/technology** (winding, stacking, formation systems); dual-use licensing. Same day: medium/heavy rare-earth controls (No. 57) and extraterritorial rare-earth rules (No. 61). [battery-energy-storage-system.com, 2025-10-14; CIRS, 2025-11-12]
- **Nov 2025**: China **temporarily suspended** (one year, per US-China truce) the Oct 9 controls on rare earths, lithium batteries, graphite anodes, diamonds etc. [CIRS Group, 2025-11-12]（2025年11月中美贸易休战后部分管制暂缓一年）
