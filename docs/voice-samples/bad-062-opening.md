# 反例：No. 062 初稿的开头

- 来源：本刊 No. 062 初稿（2026-11-11，已废弃）
- 类型：反例
- 违反规则：R1（反转句）、R2（段末升华）、R4（首段气氛铺垫）

## 原文（不要这样写）

> The United States voted on November 3. By the following afternoon, the count had produced a result that both parties had spent two years calling unlikely: the Democratic Party won both chambers of Congress.
>
> The number that now matters is not the majority. It is the margin. Overriding a presidential veto takes two-thirds of each chamber: 290 votes in the House, 67 in the Senate.

## 为什么坏

- 第一句只有时间，没有新闻。读者要知道的是「谁赢了、多少席」，不是「某国投了票」。
- 「The number that now matters is not the majority. It is the margin.」是标准的反转句骨架：先否定一个常识，再给出自己的答案。它让作者显得聪明，却不增加信息。
- 第二段结尾停在「290 与 67」这个概念上，没有落到「差 14 席」这个事实上。

## 改后

> Democrats took both chambers in the November 3 midterms: at least 224 seats in the 435-member House, at least 53 in the 100-member Senate, seven net gains where four were needed.
>
> That is a majority. It is not a veto-proof one. Overriding a president takes two-thirds of each chamber: 290 votes in the House, 67 in the Senate. The Senate result leaves the party 14 seats short.

## 差别在哪

- 第一句从「时间」换成「结果 + 数字」。
- 反转句只用一次，且由事实支撑（224 vs 290）。
- 段末落在「差 14 席」，可验证。
